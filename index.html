Career Timeline

A concise timeline of increasing scope: ramp-up, training influence, documentation ownership, L2 responsibilities, and SME development.

March 2024

Contractor Start / Rapid Ramp-Up

Started at Taco Bell Service Desk, became fully cross-trained within approximately two weeks, and quickly built proficiency across restaurant technology support areas and escalation workflows.

Mid–Late 2024

Expanded Support & Training Involvement

Learned international queues around the three-month mark, became an early consistent UK Support contributor, began formal training involvement, and created a structured shadowing/training guide from trainer feedback.

2024–2025

Cross-Training & Enablement

Helped standardize cross-training with scenario-based material targeting high call drivers, partnered on eLearning updates, and helped expand cross-training support to two days.

Early–Mid 2025

Chatbot / Agent Assist Development

Worked with leadership to create guided modules for high-impact store-facing Annspire and Tracks issues. The project was later cancelled, but it demonstrated decision-flow thinking and strong platform knowledge.

Late 2025

Operational Exposure & TKDS Training Support

Supported TKDS simulator/eLearning work before it paused and introduced veteran FTE floor-monitoring exposure to build leadership readiness and broader operational awareness.

2026

Promotion to Senior Service Desk Specialist / L2

Moved into L2 responsibilities focused on escalations, analyst development, training standardization, knowledge base improvement, and SME development.

2026 – Current

SME, AI Workflow, and International Support Development

Expanded work across TKDS/Balanced Kitchen, AI workflow KBs, Training Pod concepts, UK/vendor escalation standardization, and new ownership as the Service Desk specialist for Macromatix.
